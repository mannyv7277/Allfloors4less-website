Summer Flooring Trends For Woodland Hills Homes

Aug 5, 2026 · All Floors 4 Less

August in Woodland Hills is no joke. Between the bright Valley sunshine, triple-digit afternoons, dry air, dust, and the constant in-and-out from patios, pools, and backyards, your floors need to do more than just look good. They need to perform.

At All Floors 4 Less in Woodland Hills, CA, we help local homeowners choose flooring that fits the way they actually live—especially during our hottest months. Whether you’re updating a family room, replacing worn carpet, remodeling a kitchen, or preparing your home for guests, summer is a great time to rethink your floors with comfort, durability, and style in mind.

Below are the top summer flooring trends we’re seeing in Woodland Hills homes this August—and why choosing the right local flooring team makes all the difference.

Key Takeaways:

  • Choose heat-friendly flooring such as luxury vinyl plank, tile, engineered hardwood, or waterproof laminate for better performance during hot Woodland Hills summers.
  • Go lighter with color to create a cooler, brighter feel and reduce the appearance of dust common in dry August weather.
  • Prioritize durability if your household includes pets, kids, pool traffic, or frequent indoor-outdoor entertaining.
  • Work with a local flooring expert who understands Woodland Hills homes, climate conditions, and installation needs.

1. Light, Natural Flooring Colors Are Leading the Summer Trend

One of the biggest summer flooring trends in Woodland Hills is the shift toward lighter, natural tones. Think warm oak, sandy beige, soft greige, natural maple, and coastal-inspired wood looks. These colors pair beautifully with the sunny, open style of many Southern California homes.

In August, lighter flooring can help make your space feel cooler and more relaxed. Dark floors can look dramatic, but they often show dust, footprints, pet hair, and sunlight fading more easily—especially in homes with large windows or sliding glass doors.

Popular light flooring choices include:

  • Natural oak luxury vinyl plank
  • Light beige porcelain tile
  • Whitewashed or wire-brushed engineered hardwood
  • Soft gray waterproof laminate
  • Travertine-look tile for entryways and kitchens

These tones are especially popular in living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, and open-concept spaces where homeowners want a clean, airy look. They also work well with modern farmhouse, transitional, Mediterranean, and contemporary design styles—all common throughout Woodland Hills neighborhoods.

2. Luxury Vinyl Plank Remains a Favorite for Hot, Active Homes

If there is one product we continue to recommend for busy summer households, it’s luxury vinyl plank flooring. Today’s vinyl plank is stylish, waterproof, comfortable underfoot, and available in realistic wood and stone visuals. For Woodland Hills homeowners dealing with pool days, pets, kids, backyard barbecues, and everyday dust, it’s one of the most practical flooring choices available.

Luxury vinyl plank is especially appealing in August because it handles moisture better than traditional hardwood and is easier to maintain after summer activities. If someone walks in with wet feet from the pool or tracks dust from the patio, cleanup is simple.

Homeowners love luxury vinyl plank because it is:

  • Waterproof or highly water-resistant, depending on the product
  • Scratch-resistant for pets and active families
  • Available in realistic wood-look designs
  • Softer and quieter than tile
  • Often more budget-friendly than hardwood
  • Great for kitchens, hallways, family rooms, and bedrooms

At All Floors 4 Less, we carry a wide selection of luxury vinyl options so you can get the look you want without sacrificing performance.

“We wanted flooring that could handle our kids, our dog, and constant summer traffic from the backyard. All Floors 4 Less helped us choose a vinyl plank that looks like real wood but is so much easier to maintain.”

— Woodland Hills homeowner

3. Tile Is a Smart Choice for Keeping Homes Cool

Tile has always been popular in Southern California, and for good reason. During hot August weather in Woodland Hills, tile flooring can help create a cooler feel underfoot, especially in kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, and entryways.

Porcelain and ceramic tile are both excellent choices for homes that need durability and easy maintenance. Tile holds up well against water, spills, sand, dust, and heavy foot traffic. For homeowners who entertain during summer, tile is a great option near patio doors, pool entrances, and outdoor-adjacent areas.

This summer, we’re seeing strong interest in:

  • Large-format porcelain tile for a clean, modern look
  • Stone-look tile that mimics travertine, limestone, or marble
  • Matte finishes for better slip resistance
  • Warm neutral tones instead of cold grays
  • Patterned tile for bathrooms, laundry rooms, and accent spaces

Tile is also ideal for homeowners who want a long-lasting investment. With proper installation, tile can last for decades, making it a smart choice for high-use areas.

4. Waterproof Laminate Is Getting Better and More Beautiful

Laminate flooring has come a long way. Today’s waterproof laminate options offer impressive durability, realistic textures, and improved resistance to everyday spills. For homeowners who want the look of hardwood but need a more affordable and resilient product, waterproof laminate is a strong contender.

In Woodland Hills homes, waterproof laminate is especially popular in bedrooms, hallways, upstairs areas, and family rooms. It gives the appearance of real wood while standing up well to summer dust, shoes, pets, and daily wear.

Many customers choose laminate because it offers:

  • Attractive wood-look visuals
  • Excellent scratch resistance
  • Budget-conscious pricing
  • Comfort underfoot
  • Easy cleaning and maintenance

If you love the feel of wood flooring but want something more practical for an active summer household, waterproof laminate may be the right fit.

5. Engineered Hardwood Brings Natural Beauty with Better Stability

There is still nothing quite like the warmth and character of real wood flooring. For Woodland Hills homeowners who want genuine hardwood, engineered hardwood is often a better choice than solid hardwood, especially in areas where temperature changes and dry conditions can affect flooring materials.

Engineered hardwood is designed with a layered construction that provides greater stability than traditional solid wood. This matters in August, when air conditioning runs often and indoor humidity levels can shift. Engineered hardwood gives you the beauty of real wood with improved performance.

Trending engineered hardwood styles include:

  • Wide planks for a more upscale look
  • European oak in warm natural tones
  • Matte and low-sheen finishes
  • Wire-brushed textures that hide small scratches
  • Light brown, honey, and neutral beige finishes

Engineered hardwood is a beautiful option for living rooms, dining rooms, bedrooms, and home offices. It adds value, comfort, and timeless style to your home.

6. Pet-Friendly and Family-Friendly Floors Are a Must

Summer usually means more activity inside and outside the home. Kids are home from school, pets are running in from the yard, and guests may be visiting more often. That’s why pet-friendly and family-friendly flooring continues to be one of the most important trends for Woodland Hills homeowners.

The best floors for active homes are easy to clean, resistant to scratches, and comfortable enough for everyday living. For most families, we often recommend luxury vinyl plank, waterproof laminate, or durable tile depending on the room.

When shopping for family-friendly floors, look for:

  • Scratch-resistant wear layers
  • Waterproof or water-resistant construction
  • Textured surfaces for better traction
  • Low-maintenance cleaning requirements
  • Colors and patterns that help hide dust and daily wear

At All Floors 4 Less, we don’t just ask what style you like—we ask how you live. That helps us recommend floors that look great on day one and continue to perform long after installation.

7. Indoor-Outdoor Flow Matters in Woodland Hills Homes

Many homes in Woodland Hills are designed around indoor-outdoor living. Sliding doors, patios, pool areas, and outdoor kitchens are part of the Southern California lifestyle. Your flooring should support that flow.

For rooms that connect directly to outdoor areas, it’s important to choose flooring that can handle dirt, dust, sunlight, and occasional moisture. Tile and luxury vinyl plank are especially useful in these spaces because they are easy to clean and hold up well against summer traffic.

If your living room opens to the backyard or your kitchen connects to a patio, consider flooring that visually complements your outdoor surfaces. A natural wood-look vinyl plank, warm stone-look tile, or neutral porcelain tile can help create a smooth transition from indoors to outdoors.
