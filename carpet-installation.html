Carpet buying guide

Choosing the right carpet for your home

A few choices make a big difference in how carpet looks, feels and lasts.

Style & texture

Plush and saxony styles feel soft and look formal. Textured and frieze carpets hide footprints and vacuum marks, which makes them great for busy rooms. Berber and loop styles are durable and casual.

Fiber & padding

Nylon is known for its resilience in high-traffic areas, while polyester offers rich color and softness at a lower price. The pad underneath matters as much as the carpet: good padding adds comfort and helps the carpet last longer.

What affects the price

Your total depends on the square footage, the carpet and pad you choose, removing old flooring, stairs and any transitions. Our Instant Quote Calculator gives you a quick ballpark, and we confirm the final price after measuring.

Carpet know-how

Getting a carpet that stays comfortable

The right carpet should feel good underfoot and look good for years. These are the details that make the biggest difference after installation day.

Why padding matters

Carpet padding does more than add softness. A good pad absorbs the impact of foot traffic, which helps the carpet fibers last longer and keep their look. It also adds insulation and reduces noise between floors. Thicker is not always better: a firm, dense pad usually outperforms a thick, soft one, especially on stairs and in busy hallways. Many carpet warranties also require a specific type of pad.

Stretching and seams

A professional installation uses a power stretcher to pull the carpet tight across the room. Carpet that isn't stretched properly develops ripples and wrinkles within months. Seam placement matters too: in larger rooms, seams should be placed away from main walkways and lined up with the light, so they're as hard to spot as possible. Careful planning before cutting also reduces waste.

Choosing carpet room by room

Bedrooms and family rooms are where carpet shines, adding softness, warmth and quiet. In high-traffic areas and stairs, look for durable fibers and a tighter, lower-profile construction that resists crushing. Stain-resistant and pet-friendly carpets make sense for homes with kids and pets. Bringing home samples to see in your own light is the best way to choose a color.

Frequently asked questions

Expert carpet installation FAQs

How long does carpet installation take?

Many carpet installations are done in a day or two, depending on the number of rooms and stairs. We'll give you a timeline when we measure.

Do you remove and haul away old carpet?

Removal of your old flooring can be part of the job. Let us know when you get your estimate and we'll include it.

Can you install carpet on stairs?

Yes. Stairs take extra care and time, and we'll help you choose a carpet that wears well on steps.

Which carpet is best for pets?

Stain-resistant fibers and lower-pile or textured styles tend to hold up best with pets. We'll show you options at the showroom.

How do I care for new carpet?

Vacuum regularly, blot spills right away instead of rubbing, and schedule a professional deep cleaning periodically to keep it looking new.

Is my installation covered by a warranty?

Yes. Every installation comes with our two-year installation warranty, plus the manufacturer's warranty on your flooring materials.

How long does carpet last?

Most quality carpets last about 10 to 15 years, depending on the fiber, the padding, the amount of traffic and how well it's maintained. Regular vacuuming and occasional professional cleaning help it last longer.

Does new carpet have a smell?

Some new carpet has a mild smell for a few days. Opening windows and running fans during and after installation helps it fade quickly. Many carpets today are made with low-emission materials.

Can carpet be installed over hardwood?

Yes. Carpet can be installed over existing hardwood, but the tack strips and staples leave small holes in the wood. If you might want to uncover the hardwood later, talk to us about options first.
