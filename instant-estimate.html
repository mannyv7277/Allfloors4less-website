    Frequently asked questions

    Instant quote FAQs

    Is the instant quote my final price?

    No. It's a close estimate based on the details you enter. We confirm your final price after a free measure or a visit to our showroom.

    What isn't included in the estimate?

    Tax, delivery, major subfloor prep and baseboards are not included. Minor subfloor prep is part of a normal installation.

    Do I have to give my name or phone number?

    No. You can see your price without sharing any contact information. If you like the number, you can choose to lock it in.

    What happens after I lock in my price?

    Ray reviews your details, reaches out to confirm your price and sets up a free measure at a time that works for you.

    Does the price include removing my old floor?

    Only if you choose it. Select "Yes, remove it" and the type of floor you have, and removal is added to your estimate.
